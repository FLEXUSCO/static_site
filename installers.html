Installation expertise, in detail

01 — Substrate
Base preparation
Graded and compacted to spec before pour. Subgrade infiltration assessed; underdrain where required. No pour on unstable or wet substrate.
02 — Conditions
Pour conditions
Above 50°F, no rain within 24 hours. Temperature and wind managed at pour.
03 — Binder
Batch and pot life
Batch-managed for the working window. We mix what the crew can place — no overworked material.
04 — Placement
Pour and finish
Single-lift pour. Edges restrained before pour. Cross-slope and running slope verified before set.
05 — Edge restraint
Perimeter conditions
Hard restraint at all boundaries. Rolled or beveled edge where hard restraint isn't possible. No free-pour edges.
06 — Cure & handoff
Cure and opening
No foot traffic 24 hours. Full cure at 72 hours. Maintenance protocol delivered at handoff.
