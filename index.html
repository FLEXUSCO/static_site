Porous rubber pavement for parks and trails

Porous rubber pavement is permeable pavement for park paths and trails. Rock and recycled tire rubber are poured in place, half and half by volume, and the surface flexes.

AP is the architectural series for walking paths. Four natural palettes, Sand Dune, Coastal Granite, Charcoal Earth, and Natural Trail, keep the finish stone-forward. It is for people on foot. The Mary Esther trail at Azalea Park is AP-200.

Architectural pavementThe pavement series

Built around trees

Tree roots lift concrete sidewalks and crowd tree wells. CP is civil porous rubber pavement, poured in place to flex with the roots instead of cracking.

In Athens, Georgia, CP-200 replaced a concrete path that tree roots had displaced. The same series is used for tree wells. Like the rest of the pavement, it is for people on foot, not for vehicles.

Rubber mulch and granules

Flexus also offers its rubber on its own, outside the pavement systems. Rubber mulch and colored rubber granules come in 50 lb bags or super sacks.

Colors include Black, Forest Green, Green, Teal Green, Espresso, Brown, Cedar, Gold, and Red. Orders ship across Georgia and the Southeast. Rubber buffings ship in the same 50 lb bags, and color is usually not a choice.
