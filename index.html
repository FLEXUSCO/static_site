What it is

A poured-in-place walking surface.

Flexus sells two permeable pavement series and a separate line of rubber surfacing materials. The pavement is a continuous mix of stone aggregate and recycled tire rubber, bonded with moisture-cured polyurethane. It is for people on foot. It is not a road surface.

  • AP, architectural. Specialty aggregate palettes for parks, gardens, and other landscape work. Lifts of 1.5, 2.0, and 2.5 inches.
  • CP, civil. Granite aggregate with black recycled tire rubber for municipal trails, greenways, and sidewalks. Same lifts.
  • Who places it. Flexus can bid the full installed job, or supply the system and install guidance to your preferred contractor.
  • Rubber. The surfacing materials and the rubber in the pavement come from recycled tires.

AP and CP

Same family. Two finishes.

AP, architectural pavement

Stone-forward palettes: Sand Dune, Coastal Granite, Charcoal Earth, Natural Trail. Pedestrian paths, park circulation, and public pedestrian spaces.

CP, civil pavement

Granite aggregate and black recycled tire rubber. Municipal park trails, public greenways, and recreation paths. Athens, Georgia used CP-200 where roots had displaced concrete.
