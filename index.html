How to read our claims
Stormwater / LID / MS4
Flexus is engineered to function as a permeable pavement BMP. Acceptance is determined project-by-project by the local reviewing authority — not pre-approved by any regulatory body.
LEED
Flexus can contribute to LEED credits under Sustainable Sites and Materials & Resources. Credits are earned at the project level — no product is LEED certified on its own.
ADA
Flexus is slip-tested (ASTM E303) and designed for accessible pedestrian routes. Full compliance depends on installation, slope, and continuity — confirmed by the specifier and contractor.
