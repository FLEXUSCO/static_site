  • MixRock and recycled tire rubber, 50:50 by volume.
  • AggregateExposed natural aggregate on AP. Granite on CP.
  • InstallPoured in place.
  • UsePedestrian only.

Porous rubber pavement is permeable pavement, poured in place from rock and recycled tire rubber, half and half by volume. The surface flexes. It is for people on foot, not for vehicles.

AP and CP serve the same paths: greenways, trails, park paths, sidewalks, and tree wells.

The pavement series

Two looks

AP has a natural stone look. Four palettes: Sand Dune, Coastal Granite, Charcoal Earth, and Natural Trail. The Mary Esther trail at Azalea Park is AP-200.

CP has the familiar dark look of asphalt, a black matrix with light granite. It costs less than AP. In Athens, Georgia, CP-200 replaced a concrete path that tree roots had displaced.

Rubber mulch and granules

Flexus also offers its rubber on its own, outside the pavement systems. Rubber mulch and colored rubber granules come in 50 lb bags or super sacks.

Colors include Black, Forest Green, Green, Teal Green, Espresso, Brown, Cedar, Gold, and Red. Orders ship across Georgia and the Southeast. Rubber buffings ship in the same 50 lb bags, and color is usually not a choice.
