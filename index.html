What it is

A poured-in-place walking surface.

Flexus sells two permeable pavement series and a separate line of rubber surfacing materials. The pavement is a continuous mix of stone aggregate and recycled SBR, bonded with moisture-cured polyurethane. It is for people on foot. It is not a road surface.

  • AP, architectural. Specialty aggregate palettes for parks, gardens, and other landscape work. Lifts of 1.5, 2.0, and 2.5 inches.
  • CP, civil. Granite aggregate with black SBR for municipal trails, greenways, and sidewalks. Same lifts.
  • Who places it. Qualified partner installers. Ranger Rubber installed the Mary Esther trail. Flexus supplies the system and the install guidance.
  • Rubber. The surfacing materials and the SBR in the pavement are recycled rubber that Flexus sells. Flexus does not produce rubber.

AP and CP

Same family. Two finishes.

AP, architectural pavement

Stone-forward palettes: Sand Dune, Coastal Granite, Charcoal Earth, Natural Trail. Pedestrian paths, park circulation, and public pedestrian spaces.

CP, civil pavement

Granite aggregate and black recycled SBR. Municipal park trails, public greenways, and recreation paths. Athens, Georgia used CP-200 where roots had displaced concrete.
