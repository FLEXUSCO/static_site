Architectural pavement

Exposed natural aggregate, poured in place, with a natural stone look. Same paths as CP: greenways, trails, park paths, sidewalks, and tree wells.

From the AP cut sheet

AP and CP are used on the same paths. The lines below quote the AP cut sheet.

  • Lifts. AP-150 at 1.5 in (38 mm), AP-200 at 2.0 in (51 mm), AP-250 at 2.5 in (64 mm).
  • Finish. Textured, matte, stone-forward.
  • Binder. Moisture-cured polyurethane. Minimum 11 percent by weight.
  • Palettes. Sand Dune, Coastal Granite, Charcoal Earth, Natural Trail.
  • Use. Walking paths and trails, park circulation, public pedestrian spaces. Not for vehicles.

Install guide, in short

AP and CP serve the same paths. The AP guide names botanical gardens, zoo paths, courtyards, campus circulation, and nature preserves as example settings. Base, slope, edges, mixing, and cure are in the PDF. Compliance with accessibility rules is the designer's responsibility. The guide says a properly installed surface may support ADA slope conditions, with a recommended maximum cross slope of 2 percent.
