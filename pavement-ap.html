Pavement · AP series

Architectural pavement

Flexus AP is an architectural porous pavement for pedestrian places where a stone-forward finish matters. The cut sheet describes a 50:50 blend, by volume, of specialty aggregate and recycled tire rubber, bonded with moisture-cured polyurethane.

Specification support Download the AP PDFs

From the AP cut sheet

  • Lifts. AP-150 at 1.5 in (38 mm), AP-200 at 2.0 in (51 mm), AP-250 at 2.5 in (64 mm).
  • Finish. Textured, matte, stone-forward.
  • Binder. Moisture-cured polyurethane. Minimum 11 percent by weight.
  • Palettes. Sand Dune, Coastal Granite, Charcoal Earth, Natural Trail.
  • Use. Walking paths and trails, park circulation, public pedestrian spaces. Not for vehicles.

Install guide, in short

The AP guide covers botanical gardens, zoo paths, courtyards, campus circulation, and nature preserves as example settings. Base, slope, edges, mixing, and cure are in the PDF. Compliance with accessibility rules is the designer's responsibility. The guide says a properly installed surface may support ADA slope conditions, with a recommended maximum cross slope of 2 percent.
