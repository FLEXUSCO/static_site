Specification

Flexus pavement is an exterior walking surface. A project manual may place that work in CSI Division 32, Exterior Improvements. This site does not include a Division 32 specification.

No MasterSpec or SpecLink section is posted. Contact us for project specification support.

Stormwater

The install guides describe a surface that passes water into the base when the subgrade is prepared for it. A designer may use that section in a low-impact or municipal stormwater plan. Whether it counts as a credited best management practice, including any MS4 or runoff credit, is decided by the reviewing authority. Flexus is not pre-approved by a stormwater agency.

  • Permeability. A target greater than 200 inches per hour is cited with ASTM F1551. The report is not posted, and a measured result is not stated here.
  • Leaching. EPA 1311 (TCLP), EPA 1312 (SPLP), and RCRA metals, including mercury, are named with this surface. Those reports are not posted. This site does not restate pass or fail numbers.
  • Base. Open-graded aggregate. The reference details show a typical 4 inch ASTM No. 57 base and an optional 6 inch ASTM No. 2 subbase. The engineer of record sets the real section.

  • Posted now. Product overviews, installation guides, and PDF reference details.

Planning ranges are on the pavement systems page. They are not a bid price.

Installation

Flexus supplies the materials and the install guidance. Qualified partner installers do the work. Ranger Rubber installed the Flexus trail at Azalea Park in Mary Esther.

  • Guidance, not a substitute for the engineer. The guides cover pedestrian scope, subgrade, aggregate base, edges, weather, mixing, placement, cure, and repair. Subgrade compaction in the guides is a minimum of 95 percent Standard Proctor (ASTM D698). Recommended surface slope is 1 to 2 percent where positive drainage is required.
  • Edges. A rigid edge is described where the surface stands above adjacent grade. A beveled, backfilled edge is described where the surface is confined below grade. The PDF details say the same thing, and each sheet says it is not for construction without a registered engineer's signature.
  • Weather and cure, from the guides. Minimum ambient temperature 45°F and rising. No precipitation during installation. Initial set 4 to 8 hours. Light pedestrian traffic after 24 hours minimum. Full cure 48 to 72 hours. Working time about 20 minutes at 70°F.
  • Accessibility. The guides say a properly installed surface may support ADA slope conditions, with a recommended maximum cross slope of 2 percent. Final compliance is the designer's responsibility.
