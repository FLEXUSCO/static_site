About

Flexus Materials Ltd Co

Atlanta, Georgia. Flexus sells rubber surfacing materials and permeable pavement systems for people who specify pedestrian routes: landscape architects, park planners, and owners.

What the company sells

  • Pavement. AP architectural and CP civil systems. Poured-in-place stone and recycled tire rubber for pedestrian routes.
  • Materials. Rubber mulch, colored granules, and buffings, in 50 lb bags, separate from the pavement.
  • Installation. Flexus can bid the full installed job, or supply the system and install guidance to your preferred contractor.
