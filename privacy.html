Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Flexus Materials Ltd Co sells rubber surfacing materials and permeable pavement to businesses that specify and build pedestrian routes.

This notice explains what we collect on this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What We Collect How We Use It Who We Share It With Your Choices Contact Us

What We Collect

  • Contact and project details you send us.
  • Technical data from your browser, including analytics cookies.
  • Email we exchange with you.

The contact page has one form, handled by Netlify Forms, for specification support, a pavement sample, or a materials quote. You may give us your name, email, phone, and organization, and project details such as the product, location, quantity, and a shipping address for samples. The form also records the page, the referring page, and campaign information when those are present. Netlify stores the submission and sends a notification to our email.

When you browse, our host and measurement tools receive an IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, and the referring site. On the public site we use Google Analytics 4 with Google signals for aggregated demographics and interests reports. Before a page view is sent, the site strips sensitive URL details such as email addresses and phone numbers. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store full IP addresses. We also use Microsoft Clarity to record sessions and build heatmaps, with text masked.

If you email us, or we reply to a form, we keep that correspondence so we can answer you.

How We Use It

  • Reply to inquiries, quotes, samples, and specification requests.
  • Run, protect, and improve the site.
  • Keep ordinary business and legal records.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use it for automated decision-making that produces a legal effect, or a similar serious effect, for you.

We keep inquiry records for as long as we need them to respond, to carry on the business relationship, and to keep ordinary business records. When we no longer need them, we delete them or remove the details that identify you. Email is kept on the same basis. Google Analytics keeps event data for the retention period set in that property. This site's code does not set that period. Clarity recordings are kept under Microsoft's rules for the product.

The site is served over HTTPS, and submissions go through spam filtering. Access is limited to people who need the records for the work. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure.

Who We Share It With

  • Providers that host the site, deliver form email, and measure visits.
  • Authorities when the law requires it.
  • A buyer, if the business is transferred.

Netlify hosts the site, stores form submissions, and sends a notification to our email. Google provides Google Analytics, and Google Workspace carries our email. Microsoft provides Clarity.

We also disclose information when the law, a court, or a government request requires it, or when it is needed to protect the rights, safety, and property of Flexus, our customers, or the public.

If the business is sold, merged, or reorganized, inquiry and customer records may transfer with it. We will ask the recipient to honor this policy for those records. We do not sell personal information.

Your Choices

  • Ask for a copy, a correction, or deletion.
  • Limit analytics, including with Global Privacy Control.
  • This site is not for children.

You can also tell us not to email you. We reply to inquiries by email. We do not run a promotional mailing list. If you do not want email from us, say so and we will stop, aside from a message we still need to send to finish a request you already made.

Email info@flexusco.com, call (770) 742-6016, or mail PO Box 566001, Atlanta, GA 31156. Tell us what you are asking for. We respond within a reasonable time. We may ask for enough detail to confirm that you are the person the record is about, or that you may act for that person.

Several states, including California, give residents the right to access, correct, and delete personal information, and to opt out of sale and of sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for that advertising. You may still send a request. We will not refuse business with you because you asked. If you are outside the United States, your information is processed in the United States, where we operate the site and our email.

This site honors Global Privacy Control. When your browser sends that signal, we do not load Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity, so that visit is not measured and no session is recorded. This site does not respond to the Do Not Track setting. You can also use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, Google Ad Settings, and your browser's cookie controls. Microsoft describes Clarity in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

This site is for businesses and project teams. It is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child gave us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

We post changes on this page and update the date above. The new notice applies to visits after that date.
