Who we are

Flexus Materials Ltd Co is in Atlanta, Georgia. This policy covers our website, a business site for people who specify, buy, or install rubber surfacing materials and permeable pavement. That includes landscape architects, park planners, owners, and contractors. The site is not a consumer shop.

It describes what we collect on the site and in email about those inquiries, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us

The contact page has one form, handled by Netlify Forms. You choose a topic: specification support, a pavement sample, or a materials quote.

  • Name and email, which are required.
  • Phone and firm or organization, if you include them.
  • The topic, and any notes you write.
  • For specification support: the pavement series (AP, CP, or both) and the project location.
  • For a sample: the series, the lift, and a shipping address.
  • For a materials quote: the product (rubber mulch, colored granules, or rubber buffings), granule grade, color, quantity in 50 lb bags, ship-to ZIP, a street address if a materials sample should also ship, and whether you want a quote, a sample, or both.

The form also records the page you submitted, the first page of the visit, the referring address, and campaign identifiers when they are present, such as source, medium, campaign, and ad click ids. The firm name is stored with the submission. An empty hidden field catches automated spam. For a materials quote, the form records the unit as bags.

Netlify stores the submission. If a connection to our customer records is turned on, a site function forwards that submission to the system on the other end.

Information collected automatically

When you load a page, the host and our measurement tools receive technical data: IP address, device and browser type, the pages you open, the time of the visit, and the site that referred you. Cookies and similar identifiers are described below.

Email

If you email us, or if we reply to a form, we keep that correspondence so we can answer you and keep a record of the request.

How we use information

  • Reply to inquiries and prepare quotes, samples, and specification help.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the site.
  • Understand, in aggregate, how the site is used.
  • Protect the site and the form from spam, fraud, and abuse.
  • Meet legal duties and keep ordinary business records.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use it for automated decision-making that produces a legal effect, or a similar serious effect, for you.

Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics 4

On the public site we use Google Analytics 4 with Google signals turned on. Google signals lets Google associate a visit with a signed-in Google account when that person has allowed personalized ads. Google then produces aggregated reports about demographics and interests. Those reports do not name the account holder. We use them to understand the audience. Google explains this in how Google signals works.

Before a page view is sent, our tag removes query parameters that are not on a short allowlist, and it drops values that look like an email address or a phone number. Campaign identifiers that match an expected pattern can remain. The tag does not include a separate control that strips your IP address before Google receives the hit. Google Analytics 4 uses the IP address to estimate a coarse location and, as part of the product, does not store the full IP address in Analytics reports. Google describes its own handling in the Google Privacy Policy.

Microsoft Clarity

We use Microsoft Clarity to record sessions and to build heatmaps of how people move through the site. Text on the page is masked in those recordings. Clarity uses cookies and similar technology. Microsoft describes its handling in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Cookies and browser signals

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity set cookies so they can tell a new visit from a return visit. You can block or delete cookies in your browser. If you block them, those tools may not measure the visit. Blocking cookies does not erase data the companies have already stored.

  • Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on
  • Google Ad Settings, including controls for personalized ads
  • Microsoft Privacy Statement

This site does not look for the Global Privacy Control signal or the Do Not Track browser setting, and it does not change what it collects when a browser sends either one. Use the opt-out add-on, Google Ad Settings, and your browser's cookie controls to limit this measurement.

How we share information

We share personal information with service providers who process it for us:

  • Netlify hosts the site and stores form submissions.
  • Google provides Google Analytics, and Google Workspace carries our email.
  • Microsoft provides Clarity.

We also disclose information when the law, a court, or a government request requires it, or when it is needed to protect the rights, safety, and property of Flexus, our customers, or the public.

If the business is sold, merged, or reorganized, inquiry and customer records may transfer with it. The recipient remains bound by this policy for those records.

We do not sell personal information.

Data retention

We keep inquiry, quote, sample, and specification records for as long as we need them to respond, to carry on the business relationship, and to keep ordinary business records. When we no longer need them, we delete them or remove the details that identify you. Email is kept on the same basis.

Google Analytics keeps event data for the retention period chosen in that Google Analytics property. This site's code does not set that period. Clarity data is kept under Microsoft's rules for the product.

Data security

The site is served over HTTPS. The form includes a field that people are not meant to fill in, which reduces spam. Access to submissions is limited to people who need them for the work. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also tell us not to email you. We reply to inquiries by email. We do not run a promotional mailing list. If you do not want email from us, say so in a reply or write to us and we will stop, aside from a message we still need to send to finish a request you already made.

Email info@flexusco.com or call (770) 742-6016. Tell us what you are asking for. We respond within a reasonable time. We may ask for enough detail to confirm that you are the person the record is about, or that you may act for that person.

Several states, including California, give residents the right to access, correct, and delete personal information, and to opt out of the sale of personal information and of sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for that advertising. You may still send a request. We will not refuse business with you because you asked.

If you are outside the United States, your information is processed in the United States, where we operate the site and our email.

Children's privacy

This site is for businesses and project teams. It is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child gave us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party links

Pages and files on this site are ours. Links to other sites, including Google, Microsoft, and Netlify, leave this site. Those sites apply their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We post changes on this page and update the date at the top. The new policy applies to visits after that date.
