Pavement · CP series

Civil pavement

Flexus CP is a civil porous pavement for public walkways. The cut sheet describes granite aggregate and recycled SBR, with black SBR granules, bonded with moisture-cured polyurethane.

Specification support Download the CP PDFs

From the CP cut sheet

  • Lifts. CP-150 at 1.5 in (38 mm), CP-200 at 2.0 in (51 mm), CP-250 at 2.5 in (64 mm).
  • Finish. Textured, exposed granite aggregate.
  • Binder. Moisture-cured polyurethane. Minimum 11 percent by weight. The install guide gives a CP-200 example of about 10.34 lb of solids per square foot and binder at 11 to 12.5 percent by weight of total solids.
  • Use. Municipal park trails, public greenways, recreation paths. The install guide also names sidewalks, campus circulation, and municipal walkways. Not for vehicles.
Athens project notes

Athens, Georgia

Flexus CP-200 replaced a concrete path that tree root intrusion had displaced.

The CP cut sheet says the surface is suitable for ADA-aligned pedestrian routes when it is installed over a compliant subbase. The install guide says final accessibility compliance is the project designer's responsibility.
