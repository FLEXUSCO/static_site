Pavement systems

AP and CP permeable pavement

Both series are poured in place, about half stone and half recycled tire rubber by volume, with a moisture-cured polyurethane binder at a minimum of 11 percent by weight. Installed lifts are 1.5, 2.0, and 2.5 inches. Pedestrian use only.

Limits that apply to both

  • Pedestrian only. Not designed for vehicular, emergency, or maintenance equipment unless a project is specifically engineered for that load.
  • Site dependent. Final performance depends on site conditions, substrate preparation, and construction. A stable, well-drained subgrade is required.
  • Who installs. Flexus can bid the full installed job, or supply the system and install guidance to your preferred contractor.
